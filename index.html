Clippers vs Nuggets: Game 6

The Los Angeles Clippers face elimination as they host the Denver Nuggets in a crucial Game 6 of their playoff series.

Game Summary

The Los Angeles Clippers are facing the Denver Nuggets in Game 6 on Thursday night at the Intuit Dome. Denver currently leads the series 3-2 after winning Game 5 with a score of 131-115.

In Game 5, Jamal Murray led the Nuggets with an impressive 43 points, while Ivica Zubac led the Clippers with a playoff career-high of 27 points.

To avoid elimination, the Clippers must win Game 6 at home. If they manage to secure a victory, the decisive Game 7 will take place on Saturday in Denver.

The Clippers will need to find a way to contain Murray, who has been on fire throughout the series, while also getting more production from their star players.
